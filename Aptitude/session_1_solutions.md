Worked solutions for every question in the deck, in slide order. Each one uses the shortcut taught in the session.

## Diagnostic Speed Test (Slides 4–5)

**Q1. 37.5% of 480 = ?**
37.5% = 3/8 → 3/8 × 480 = **180**

**Q2. A number rises 25%, then falls 20%. Net change?**
a + b + ab/100 = 25 − 20 + (25 × −20)/100 = 25 − 20 − 5 = **0% (no change)**

**Q3. Marked 50% above cost, sold at 20% discount. Profit %?**
Multipliers: 1.5 × 0.8 = 1.2 → **20% profit**

**Q4. A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Find A : B : C.**
Make B common: LCM of 3 and 4 = 12
A : B = 8 : 12, B : C = 12 : 15 → **A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15**

**Q5. CI − SI on ₹20,000 at 5% p.a. for 2 years = ?**
P × (r/100)² = 20,000 × (0.05)² = 20,000 × 0.0025 = **₹50**

**Q6. Tea at ₹120/kg mixed with tea at ₹150/kg to get ₹130/kg. Ratio?**
Alligation: cheaper parts = 150 − 130 = 20, dearer parts = 130 − 120 = 10
**20 : 10 = 2 : 1** (cheaper : dearer)

**Q7. 98 × 102 = ?**
Around a middle number: (100 − 2)(100 + 2) = 100² − 2² = 10,000 − 4 = **9,996**

**Q8. Salary grew 10% a year for 2 years to ₹48,400. Original salary?**
Multiplier for 2 years = 1.1 × 1.1 = 1.21
Original = 48,400 ÷ 1.21 = **₹40,000**

**Q9. Sugar price rises 25%. Cut consumption by what % to keep spend the same?**
Reduction = r / (100 + r) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = **20%**
(Fraction view: price × 5/4, so consumption × 4/5, a cut of 1/5.)

**Q10. 16.67% of 726 + 12.5% of 640 = ?**
1/6 × 726 = 121, 1/8 × 640 = 80 → 121 + 80 = **201**

## "Your Turn" Questions (Slides 9–13)

**Slide 9: Successive change, +25% then −20% = ?**
25 − 20 − (25 × 20)/100 = 25 − 20 − 5 = **0% (no change)**

**Slide 10: Marked 60% above cost, 25% discount. Profit %?**
1.6 × 0.75 = 1.20 → **20% profit**

**Slide 11: A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 5 : 7. A : B : C = ?**
Make B common: LCM of 3 and 5 = 15
A : B = 10 : 15, B : C = 15 : 21 → **A : B : C = 10 : 15 : 21**

**Slide 12: CI − SI on ₹20,000 at 5% for 2 years = ?**
20,000 × (5/100)² = 20,000 × 0.0025 = **₹50**

**Slide 13: Milk at ₹60/L mixed with water (₹0/L) to sell at ₹50/L. Milk : water?**
Alligation: milk parts = 50 − 0 = 50, water parts = 60 − 50 = 10
**50 : 10 = 5 : 1**

## Graded Practice, Level 1 (Slide 17)

**Q1. Marked 30% above cost, sold at 10% discount. Profit %?**
1.3 × 0.9 = 1.17 → **17% profit**

**Q2. Population of 50,000 grows 10% in year 1, falls 10% in year 2. Final population?**
Net change = 10 − 10 − (10 × 10)/100 = −1%
50,000 × 0.99 = **49,500**

**Q3. ₹2,600 divided among A, B, C in the ratio ½ : ⅓ : ¼. A's share?**
Multiply by LCM 12: ½ : ⅓ : ¼ = 6 : 4 : 3 (total 13 parts)
A = 6/13 × 2,600 = **₹1,200**

**Q4. A's income is 25% more than B's. B's income is what % less than A's?**
r / (100 + r) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = **20% less**
(Fraction view: A = 5/4 of B, so B = 4/5 of A.)

## Graded Practice, Level 2 (Slide 18)

**Q5. On ₹5,000: two discounts of 20% and 10%, or one of 30%? Difference in price?**
Successive: −20 − 10 + (20 × 10)/100 = −28% → price = ₹3,600
Single 30% → price = ₹3,500
Difference = 2% of 5,000 = **₹100** (the single 30% discount is better for the buyer)

**Q6. Shopkeeper uses a 900 g weight for 1 kg and sells at cost price. Gain %?**
Gain = error / true weight given × 100 = 100/900 × 100 = **11.11%** (= 1/9)

**Q7. 20 L of milk and water in ratio 3 : 1. How much water to add to make it 3 : 2?**
Milk = 15 L, water = 5 L. Milk stays the same.
New ratio 3 : 2 → water = 15 × 2/3 = 10 L
Water to add = 10 − 5 = **5 L**

**Q8. A sum doubles in 5 years at compound interest. When will it become 8 times?**
8 = 2³, so it takes 3 doubling periods: 3 × 5 = **15 years**
