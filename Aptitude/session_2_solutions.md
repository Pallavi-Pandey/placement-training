# Session 2 Solutions: Time-Work & Time-Speed-Distance

Worked solutions for every question in the deck, in slide order. Each one uses the framework taught in the session.

## Diagnostic Speed Test — Time & Work, Pipes (Slide 4)

**Q1. A finishes a job in 15 days, B in 10 days. Together?**
1/15 + 1/10 = 2/30 + 3/30 = 5/30 = 1/6 → **6 days**

**Q2. 6 people finish a job in 10 days. How many days for 15 people?**
Man-days = 6 × 10 = 60 → 60 ÷ 15 = **4 days**

**Q3. A is twice as efficient as B. B alone takes 24 days. Together?**
B = 1/24, A = 2/24 → together = 3/24 = 1/8 → **8 days**

**Q4. Pipe A fills a tank in 12 min. Pipe B empties it in 18 min. Both open?**
Net rate = 1/12 − 1/18 = 3/36 − 2/36 = 1/36 → **36 minutes**

**Q5. Two pipes fill a tank in 20 h and 30 h. Together?**
1/20 + 1/30 = 3/60 + 2/60 = 5/60 = 1/12 → **12 hours**

## Diagnostic Speed Test — Speed, Trains, Boats (Slide 5)

**Q6. 90 km/h = ? m/s**
90 × 5/18 = **25 m/s**

**Q7. A 180 m train runs at 54 km/h. Time to cross a pole?**
54 km/h = 15 m/s → 180 ÷ 15 = **12 s**

**Q8. Trains of 120 m and 180 m run in opposite directions at 40 and 50 km/h. Time to cross each other?**
Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s; total length = 300 m → 300 ÷ 25 = **12 s**

**Q9. Boat 12 km/h in still water, stream 4 km/h. Time for 24 km upstream?**
Upstream speed = 12 − 4 = 8 km/h → 24 ÷ 8 = **3 h**

**Q10. You drive to your Bengaluru office at 30 km/h and back at 60 km/h. Average speed?**
2xy/(x+y) = 2 × 30 × 60 ÷ 90 = 3,600 ÷ 90 = **40 km/h**

## Framework 1: Think in Units, Not Fractions (Slide 7)

**Your turn: A finishes a job in 10 days, B in 15 days. How long together?**
Total = LCM(10, 15) = 30 units. A = 3/day, B = 2/day → sum = 5/day
30 ÷ 5 = **6 days**

---

## Framework 2: Man-Days — Balance Both Sides (Slide 8)

**Your turn: 10 packers clear 400 orders in 4 hours. How many packers clear 600 orders in 3 hours?**
M₁D₁H₁ ÷ W₁ = M₂D₂H₂ ÷ W₂ → 10 × 4 ÷ 400 = P × 3 ÷ 600
10 × 4 × 600 = P × 3 × 400 → 24,000 = 1,200P → **P = 20 packers**

---

## Framework 3: When Someone Leaves or Joins (Slide 9)

**Your turn: A takes 10 days, B 20 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. How many more days for B?**
Total = LCM(10, 20) = 20 units. A = 2/day, B = 1/day
4 days together = (2 + 1) × 4 = 12 units done → remaining = 8 units
B alone: 8 ÷ 1 = **8 more days**

---

## Framework 4: Pipes & Cisterns — Inlets Add, Outlets Subtract (Slide 10)

**Your turn: A tap fills a tank in 6 h. Because of a leak it takes 8 h. How long does the leak alone take to empty the full tank?**
Leak rate = fill rate − net rate = 1/6 − 1/8 = 4/24 − 3/24 = 1/24 → **24 hours**

## Game: Units Race (Slide 11)

**A: 6 days, B: 12 days. Together?**
1/6 + 1/12 = 2/12 + 1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4 → **4 days**

**A: 15, B: 10, C: 30 days. All three?**
LCM = 30. A = 2/day, B = 3/day, C = 1/day → sum = 6/day → 30 ÷ 6 = **5 days**

**Pipe fills in 4 h, outlet empties in 6 h. Both open?**
1/4 − 1/6 = 3/12 − 2/12 = 1/12 → **12 hours**

**A is twice as fast as B. Together 10 days. B alone?**
B = x, A = 2x → 3x = 1/10 → x = 1/30 → **B alone = 30 days**

## Framework 5: Speed Basics and Average Speed (Slide 12)

**Your turn: You cycle to college at 12 km/h and back at 18 km/h. Average speed?**
2xy/(x+y) = 2 × 12 × 18 ÷ 30 = 432 ÷ 30 = **14.4 km/h**

## Framework 6: Relative Speed — Add or Subtract (Slide 13)

**Your turn: Rahul leaves at 9:00 at 40 km/h. Priya follows on the same road at 9:30 at 60 km/h. When does she catch him?**
Rahul's head start by 9:30 = 40 × 0.5 = 20 km
Relative speed (same direction) = 60 − 40 = 20 km/h
Time to catch up = 20 ÷ 20 = 1 h after 9:30 → **catches him at 10:30**

## Framework 7: Trains — What Length, Which Speed (Slide 14)

**Your turn: Trains of 200 m and 250 m run in opposite directions at 50 and 40 km/h. Time to cross each other?**
Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s; total length = 450 m → 450 ÷ 25 = **18 s**

## Framework 8: Boats & Streams (Slide 15)

**Your turn: A boat covers 24 km downstream in 2 h and the same 24 km upstream in 3 h. Speed of the stream?**
Downstream speed = 24 ÷ 2 = 12 km/h, upstream speed = 24 ÷ 3 = 8 km/h
Stream = (12 − 8) ÷ 2 = **2 km/h**

**72 km/h = ? m/s**

## Graded Practice, Level 1 (Slide 18)

**Q1. A can do a job in 8 days, B in 12 days. How long together?**
1/8 + 1/12 = 3/24 + 2/24 = 5/24 → **24/5 = 4.8 days**

**Q2. Two pipes fill a tank in 20 and 30 min; an outlet empties it in 15 min. All open, time to fill?**
1/20 + 1/30 − 1/15 = 3/60 + 2/60 − 4/60 = 1/60 → **60 minutes**

**Q3. A 240 m train at 72 km/h crosses a 160 m bridge. Time taken?**
72 km/h = 20 m/s; distance = 240 + 160 = 400 m → 400 ÷ 20 = **20 s**

**Q4. Boat speed 10 km/h in still water, stream 2 km/h. Time for 48 km downstream?**
Downstream speed = 12 km/h → 48 ÷ 12 = **4 h**

## Graded Practice, Level 2 (Slide 19)

**Q5. A + B finish in 12 days, B + C in 15, A + C in 20. How long for A, B and C together?**
2(A+B+C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 5/60 + 4/60 + 3/60 = 12/60 = 1/5
A+B+C = 1/10 → **10 days**

**Q6. A is 50% more efficient than B. Together they finish in 18 days. A alone?**
B = x, A = 1.5x → 2.5x = 1/18 → x = 1/45
A's rate = 1.5/45 = 1/30 → **A alone = 30 days**

**Q7. A train passes a pole in 15 s and a 100 m platform in 25 s. Length of the train?**
L = 15s (pole) and L + 100 = 25s (platform) → 15s + 100 = 25s → 100 = 10s → s = 10 m/s
L = 15 × 10 = **150 m**

**Q8. A man rows 48 km and back in 14 h. He rows 4 km downstream in the time he rows 3 km upstream. Stream speed?**
Let downstream speed = D, upstream speed = U.
4/D = 3/U → U = 3D/4
48/D + 48/U = 14 → 48/D + 64/D = 112/D = 14 → D = 8 km/h, U = 6 km/h
Stream = (D − U) ÷ 2 = (8 − 6) ÷ 2 = **1 km/h**
